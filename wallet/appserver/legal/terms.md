## You control your keys

If you lose your seed and your device, your funds are lost permanently. Write your seed down and keep it somewhere safe. Anyone who has your seed controls your funds.

## What Caribou does

Caribou lets you hold, send, and receive supported assets, and swap between them. It talks directly to public blockchain networks and to third-party services on your behalf. We do not operate a server that holds your funds, processes your transactions, or stores your account.

## Transactions are final

Blockchain transactions cannot be reversed or cancelled once broadcast. We cannot refund, cancel, or recover a transaction. Check every address and amount before you confirm. Sending to a wrong address, the wrong network, or an unsupported token can lose your funds.

## Swaps

Swaps are executed by third-party services, currently NEAR Intents, not by us. A swap quote can change or expire, and a swap can fail or be refunded by the provider. The amount you receive can be lower than the quote shows if the provider's conditions change. Caribou charges an application fee on swaps. The fee is included in the quote and is shown in the swap screen before you confirm. Network fees and provider fees are separate and are paid to the networks and the provider.
